OUR MISSION AND CULTURE

EYE WORLDZ was created to celebrate the extreme sports and street culture that make Los Angeles unique. Our website focuses on the passion, creativity, and determination of people who express themselves through skateboarding, BMX, surfing, fixed gear cycling, graffiti, and car culture. We believe these activities represent more than entertainment. They are ways for individuals to challenge themselves, develop skills, and build connections within their communities.

Los Angeles has a strong street culture influenced by its neighborhoods, artists, athletes, and creative communities. Skateboarding and BMX riders use urban spaces to practice tricks and develop their own styles. Surfers enjoy the California coastline, while fixed gear cyclists explore the city on two wheels. Graffiti artists contribute to the visual identity of the streets, and car enthusiasts showcase their creativity through customized vehicles and automotive culture.

Our goal is to bring these different communities together through photography, videos, and interesting stories. EYE WORLDZ gives visitors an opportunity to discover the personalities, environments, and lifestyles behind these activities. We want our website to capture the energy of Los Angeles while encouraging people to appreciate the creativity and dedication involved in extreme sports. Whether someone is an experienced athlete or simply interested in street culture, our website provides a place to explore new perspectives and discover what makes these communities exciting.
